**Mock Interview Questions**

**General Questions**

 Tell us about yourself

 Why did you choose to study your current degree?

 What are your biggest strengths?

 What is your biggest weakness?

 What is your proudest achievement?

 What are three words your peers would use to describe you and why?

 Tell us about something that you have pursued outside of your studies? What was it and

how does it add to your life or skills?

 Where would you like to see yourself in 5 years?

 What motivates you?

**Behavioural Questions**

 Tell me about a time when you had to utilise strong **organisational skills.**

 Provide an example of how you have utilised your **communication skills** to get an

important message across.

 Describe an experience you have had of working in a successful **team**

 Describe a complex **problem** you have solved and how you went about it.

 Describe a time when you had to juggle **multiple deadlines** or projects at the same time

 Tell me about a time you had to work with a **difficult customer or client**. How did you

respond? What was the outcome?

 Tell me about a time when you have had to pay high **attention to detail** to record

information. How did you ensure you were accurate?

 Give an example of a time when there was a **decision** to be made, but no procedures in

place. What did you do?

 Describe a time when you were involved in a **group project** and a problem arose. How

did you approach it? How was it solved?

 Describe a time when you had little guidance and had to **come up with a solution**?

What did you do?
