# 31275 [[Mobile Networking]]

## Subject Information

**Delivery**: Spring Session  
**Total workload hours**: 150 hours  
**Credit points**: 6  
**Result type**: Grade and marks

---

## Subject Description

Wireless networks — including [[cellular]], [[WiFi]], [[Bluetooth]], [[satellite]], and [[near-field communication (NFC)]] — are foundational technologies across industries.  
This subject covers principles and applications of wireless telecommunications, emphasizing infrastructure, protocols, and the support of diverse mobility needs. Guest lectures from major telecoms and practical group projects provide hands-on experience and industry insight.

---

## Subject Learning Outcomes (SLOs)

1. Explain concepts, techniques, and protocols in wireless networks to support wireless technologies. (D.1)  
2. Analyze infrastructure requirements for mobile devices and users. (B.1)  
3. Develop and showcase innovative wireless solutions. (C.1)  
4. Solve integrated [[mobile networking]] problems using theory and standards. (D.1)

---

## Teaching and Learning Strategies

- **3 hours weekly**: Lecture + Tutorial  
- **Two stages**:
  - **Weeks 1–9**: Theory — pre-class online lectures, weekly tasks, quizzes, discussions
  - **Weeks 10–12**: Project development — group work with themes: cooperation, competition, commercialization
- **Use of AI tools** (e.g., Microsoft Copilot) encouraged with critical oversight and integrity
- Feedback is continuous: oral, written, and peer-based

### [[Mobile Networking Assessment task 2 Mobile Application Design project]]

- **Groupwork**: Group (3 students), peer-assessed

- **Learning Outcomes**: SLO2, SLO3
