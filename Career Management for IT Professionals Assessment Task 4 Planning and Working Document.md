# Planning
## **Describe**

- The scenario: asked to pitch an IT infrastructure upgrade to the client’s CEO, CTO, and other senior leaders.
    
- What the pitch included: upgrading 35 devices, consolidating servers and NAS into a large VM-hosted solution with backup.
    
- Steps: researching solutions → presenting to your team and boss → final pitch to the client.
    

## **Interpret**

- Why this mattered:
    
    - First time presenting such technical recommendations at a high level.
        
    - Needed to translate **complex IT concepts** into business value (ROI, efficiency, security, scalability).
        
    - Realised that being an IT professional isn’t just technical — it’s also about **communication and persuasion**.
        
- Connection to the IT industry:
    
    - Professionals must bridge the gap between **technical solutions** and **business needs**.
        
    - Decision-makers rarely care about specs — they care about **outcomes** (cost savings, productivity, risk reduction).
        

## **Evaluate**

- What went well:
    
    - Research was thorough; solution was cost-effective and scalable.
        
    - Successfully tailored language so executives could understand value.
        
    - Boosted confidence in presenting technical proposals.
        
- What was challenging:
    
    - Balancing technical detail vs. business-level communication.
        
    - Feeling pressure in front of high-level stakeholders.
        
    - Could have prepared more visual aids/financial comparisons.
        
- Literature / theory link: could connect to “professional communication in IT”, “stakeholder management”, or even agile/DevOps values (continuous improvement, adaptability).
    

## **Plan**

- Future applications:
    
    - Develop more skills in **business case writing** and financial justification for IT solutions.
        
    - Practise **tailoring communication** for different audiences (technical peers vs. executives).
        
    - Explore professional development in presentation skills, technical writing, and client engagement.
        
- Long-term:
    
    - Aim to become an IT professional who not only implements solutions but also influences **strategic business decisions**.
