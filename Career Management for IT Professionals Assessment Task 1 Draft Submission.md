Lanrex is a leading IT Managed Services Provider with extensive experience in consulting, solution design and managed services to the mid-market and small-medium enterprise sectors, with offices in Sydney - Macquarie Park, supported by Tier 1 partnerships across major vendors and distributors, and a highly skilled professional team. Lanrex has a reputation for delivering independent consulting advice, high-quality technical services and great client experience.  
  
**We seek an experienced ICT Support Specialist (Level 2) based in Sydney - Macquarie Park.**

Join our team as an ICT Support Specialist, where you’ll play a crucial role in responding to client incidents and escalations with the larger Support team. You will be responsible for maintaining the integrity and optimising our clients’ IT support experience. You’ll handle escalated technical issues from end-users or other IT teams relating to all technology, both on-premise and cloud technology. Collaborate with our Customer Success Team and Optimise Project Team and ensure seamless delivery of managed services.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Reactive IT Support, first & second IT Support relating to customer devices, systems and network infrastructure issues.
    
- Support services for Microsoft technologies: Microsoft Azure services, Windows Server, Exchange, SQL, SharePoint, Cloud Services, Office 365, Intune Infrastructure.
    
- Be responsible for the deployment lifecycle of our client's laptops, utilising Intune and Autopilot
    
- Support services for virtualisation technologies: VMware and Microsoft.
    
- Remote access solution support includes VPN, Terminal Services, Citrix XenApp, and VDA.
    
- Technical services and support at the network level: WAN and LAN connectivity, routers, firewalls, internet, telecommunications, and security.
    
- Responding to security alerts and remediating security-related incidents and issues. 
    
- Communication with stakeholders as required: keeping the customers informed of incident progress and notifying them of impending changes or agreed-upon outages.
    
- Looking to continually improve and optimise the Lanrex RMM monitoring system. 
    
- Streamline processes leveraging automation. 
    

**Benefits of this role include:**

- Opportunity to draw upon existing Support and NOC team leadership while working with a multi-region service desk engineering team.
    
- Opportunity to work with highly skilled systems engineers, architects and service desk teams to achieve customer service outcomes.
    
- Opportunity to expand your technical knowledge and experience within a growing provider.
    
- Opportunity to develop in higher-level technical services and project delivery. 
    
- Intentional Professional Development Plans for continued learning.
    
- A large suite of technical tools to support the team's ability to deliver a holistic Support Services function.
    

**Professional Skills and Experience Requirements:**

- 2+ years in computer support, network, IT end-user and infrastructure support.
    
- Experience in supporting Microsoft core services, including Azure, Cloud Services, Exchange, Office 365, Active Directory, SQL Server, Cloud Services, O365, Intune and Windows Server 2016 systems administration.
    
- Exposure across server virtualisation technologies. (VMware, Microsoft preferred)
    
- Exposure to supporting backup, Storagecraft, Azure backup and Datto products is highly desirable. 
    
- Advanced customer service and communication experience.
    
- Previous Managed Services experience in the Mid-Tier/SME sector is well regarded.

- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to liaise effectively with client representatives at all levels.
