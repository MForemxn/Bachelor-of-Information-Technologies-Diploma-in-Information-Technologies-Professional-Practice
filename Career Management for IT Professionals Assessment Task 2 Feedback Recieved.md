### Summary of Feedback
This should be no more than 2-3 paragraphs indicating the feedback you received on your interview. If you did not receive any feedback, please indicate why and self-reflect on what you could improve in your interview skills.

**My feedback summary:**  
From the feedback I received, one of my main strengths was the clear, structured way I used STAR to answer each question. Both reviewers noted that I communicated calmly under pressure and broke down my approach logically, which demonstrated professionalism and problem-solving ability. They also highlighted that my emphasis on prioritization, communication, and methodical troubleshooting came across strongly, showing that I can stay composed and effective even in high-stakes scenarios.  

Areas for improvement were around providing more measurable outcomes and being more specific about my individual contributions. I was encouraged to keep the “Situation” concise and spend more time highlighting the impact of my actions. For the hypothetical coding scenario, the suggestion was to phrase my answer more confidently — explaining step by step what I would do — and to connect it more directly to transferable skills from my IT support role.  

Overall, the feedback was positive and constructive. I plan to continue using structured answers while also incorporating clearer metrics, stronger ownership of my role, and linking hypothetical answers back to my real-world experience. This will help me sound more confident, impactful, and relatable in future interviews.
